Classroom Tips

👏 Echo

You clap and say 4 beats; the class echoes. Keep a steady beat with your foot or the generator.

👀 Read

Project a pattern. Say it in syllables first, then clap it. Rests: open hands, no sound.

🛠️ Create

Rhythm Engineers build their own 4-beat pattern, then teach it to a partner or the class.
