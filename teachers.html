Rhythm Syllables

Levels 1–4

ti-ti (“tee-tee”)

Levels 1–4

tika-tika

rest

Levels 2–4

Classroom Tips

👏 Echo

You clap and say 4 beats; the class echoes. Keep a steady beat with your foot or the generator.

👀 Read

Project a pattern. Say it in syllables first, then clap it. Rests: open hands, no sound.

🛠️ Create

Rhythm Engineers build their own 4-beat pattern, then teach it to a partner or the class.

Standards We Follow

Every Music Quests lesson is aligned to the National Core Arts Standards for Music (2014), which the Illinois Arts Learning Standards are built on. We follow them so teachers can drop Beat Bots straight into their curriculum and know exactly which skills students are practicing: reading, performing, creating, and responding to rhythm. Codes end in the grade (1–6) and a component letter, so pick the grade you teach.

Levels 1–3 (videos)

  • MU:Pr4.2.1a – steady beat
  • MU:Pr4.2.1b–5b – read and perform rhythm patterns (ta, ti-ti, rest)
  • MU:Pr6.1.1a–6a – perform with accuracy
  • MU:Re7.2.1a / 2a – describe how beat and rhythm are used
